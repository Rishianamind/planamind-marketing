Platform Capabilities

Everything your planning
team needs, unified.

From statistical forecasting to supply replenishment — one platform, one source of truth, built for the way planners actually work.

📈

Statistical Forecasting

Automated baseline generation using best-fit statistical models. Handles seasonality, trends, and intermittent demand patterns across your full product hierarchy.

7-Level Product Hierarchy

Model your business exactly as it is — from Division to Pack level. Forecast and plan at any level, roll up or drill down instantly.

S&OP Workflow

Structured demand review, supply review, and executive consensus cycles. Version-controlled plans with project-level data isolation.

Supply Planning

Net requirements calculation, safety stock logic, and replenishment orders — automatically derived from your approved demand plan.

Customer Dimension

Channel and customer-level forecasting built in. Plan by region, customer, or channel — not just aggregate volumes.
