Why planners are stuck

Business planning is broken
in four predictable ways.

And every one of them shows up in the next quarterly review.

1

Tool sprawl

Demand in one system, supply in another, finance in a third, dashboards in a fourth. Every cycle is reconciled in email and Excel. Reality lives in spreadsheets nobody can audit.

2

Implementation paralysis

Incumbent deployments run 6–24 months and burn millions before the first planner logs in. Half never reach steady-state — and the SI invoice still arrives.

3

Knowledge that walks out

Senior planners hold the heuristics. When they leave, the institution starts over. Replacement training takes 9–18 months, with stockouts in between.

4

AI theatre

Every legacy vendor now ships an "AI assistant" that mostly rewords a chart caption. None of them pre-empt the next planning decision in your SKU codes.

Why now

Three shifts have made the old
planning playbook untenable.

And the cost of staying on it now compounds quarter on quarter.

Volatility is the new baseline

Post-COVID supply shocks, geopolitical disruption, and climate-led demand swings have turned monthly cycles into weekly fire-fighting. Models built for steady-state demand miss too often.

Working capital under pressure

Inflation and rate cycles have made every dollar of inventory more expensive to carry. CFOs are pushing for safety stock that is defensible — not whatever the planner has done for years.

AI cleared the cost barrier

12-model ensembles and conversational AI used to need a data-science team. Today they cost cents per SKU and ship as software. Vendors that don't pass that saving on are about to look very expensive.

The intelligence layer

Seven planning intelligences
your current tool doesn't have.

None of these require a data-science team, a consultant, or a second platform. They ship in Planamind on day one.

1

12-Model AutoML

SES, Holt-Winters, Theta, ARIMA, Prophet-lite, MLP, Neural Network, Ensemble, Croston — not just the 3 models your incumbent ships. Auto-picks the winner per SKU on MAPE, with manual override and instant visual feedback.

2

Forecast Accuracy Recommendations

Classifies every SKU by pattern — intermittent, biased, wrong-model, volatile, or healthy — and ranks improvements by expected portfolio FA gain. Each rec is pre-validated on a 6-month holdout before it surfaces.

3

Safety Stock Intelligence

Recommends optimal safety-stock days per SKU using your ABC service-level targets (98/95/90). Shows recommended vs. current as a Δ column. The planner adopts in one click.

4

New Product Analogies

Scores candidate reference SKUs by hierarchy similarity, declining trend, and volume match — and produces a plain-English reasoning narrative, not just a code. Feeds the forecast engine from day one.

5

External Signal Regression

Auto-correlates macro indicators (PMI, CPI), weather, and cost series against your demand. Surfaces R², lag, p-value, and confidence bands so planners see statistical evidence before acting.

6

Events Library & Elasticity

Models historical event impact — promotions, price changes, holidays — with elasticity coefficients and a Headroom KPI. Year-over-year event attribution is built in.

7

Monthly Review Narrative

Ana explains last month's performance against forecast and AOP, attributing the move to internal causes (model selection, overrides, channel mix) and external factors (macro signals, events). FY risks and opportunities surface automatically every cycle.
