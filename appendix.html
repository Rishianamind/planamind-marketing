Built-in AI

Ana — the planning copilot
in every screen.

Not a sidebar chatbot. Embedded across Demand, Supply, Finance, Analytics, Signals, and BI — grounded in your real SKU codes.

What Ana does

  • Answers in 3–5 sentences, grounded in your real SKU codes.
  • Generates the Business Report — deterministic numbers plus an executive summary.
  • Generates the Analytics Report — 4–6 "what's working / why / watch" cards.
  • Discovers competitors and feeds the news view automatically.
  • Surfaces screen-aware suggested questions for every module.

What Ana will not do

  • Off-topic queries are deflected before any AI call — zero cost.

SharePoint sync

Drop files in SharePoint.
Planamind picks them up.

For customers whose planning files already live in a Document Library — no manual re-uploads.

STEP 1

Drop file in SharePoint

Planner adds Excel to the Document Library — exactly as today.

STEP 2

Planamind reads via Graph

Microsoft Graph, Sites.Selected (per-site grant), Files.Read.All. Read-only.

STEP 3

Filename routes to processor

Master, transactions, supply, pricelist, budget, BOM — auto-classified.

STEP 4

Processed & surfaced

Errors surface as structured issues. No raw stack traces to planners.

What IT sees

  • Standard Azure AD app registration — no on-prem agent.
  • Sites.Selected (per-site grant only) + Files.Read.All. No Delegated permissions, no user impersonation.
  • Read-only by design — Planamind never writes back to SharePoint.
  • Client secrets stored AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest.

Operationally

  • Rotation is a 30-second operation in Settings.
  • 30 minutes from Azure registration to first sync.
  • On-demand or scheduled poll — your choice.
